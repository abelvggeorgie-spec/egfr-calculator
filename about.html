G5: Kidney Failure< 15

About the 2021 Equation

The CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine equation estimated glomerular filtration rate without a race coefficient. Developed by the Chronic Kidney Disease Epidemiology Collaboration, it aims to deliver equitable care calculations across diverse clinical groups.
